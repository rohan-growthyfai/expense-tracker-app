Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026

Spendly is a personal expense tracker, so nearly everything it holds is information about your own money. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and what we do with it.

Data We Collect

We keep the collection deliberately narrow. Spendly stores only what it needs in order to work:

  • Account details — the email address and password you register with. Passwords are stored in hashed form, never as plain text.
  • Expense records — the amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter yourself.
  • Basic technical data — the standard information a web server records, such as the time of a request, so the service can run and stay secure.

We do not ask for and do not collect your bank credentials, card numbers, or account statements. Spendly only ever knows what you type into it.

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to provide the service back to you — signing you in, saving your expenses, and producing the category breakdowns, filters, and monthly summaries you see in the app.

We do not sell your data, rent it, or share it with advertisers. We do not use your expense records to build advertising profiles. If we ever needed to use your information for a purpose not described here, we would ask you first.

Data Storage

Your account and expense records are stored in the application's database. We take reasonable technical measures to protect them, including hashing passwords and restricting access to stored records.

No method of storage or transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. We keep your data for as long as your account is active; if you close your account, the associated records are removed.

Third Party Services

Spendly keeps outside dependencies to a minimum. The application loads web fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google's servers to fetch those font files. We also rely on a hosting provider to run the application and store its database.

These providers process data only as needed to deliver their part of the service. We do not pass your expense records to any third party for marketing, analytics, or profiling.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy, want to know what data we hold about you, or would like your account and records deleted, get in touch at privacy@spendly.app and we will respond as soon as we can.

This policy may be updated as the service develops. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "last updated" date. Your use of Spendly is also governed by our Terms and Conditions.
